# Super-Resolution Preference Study

A short online study on how people judge the quality of enlarged (super-resolved) photographs. Each screen
shows two versions of the same image region and you pick one. It takes about 12 minutes.

**Take part:** https://devesh-deepak.github.io/SR_Preference_Study/

Choices and response times are recorded anonymously for research. No name or email is collected.

The image crops come from the public research datasets RealSR, DRealSR and DIV2K and are shown for academic
research only.
